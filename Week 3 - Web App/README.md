# Week 3 - ADHD Budgeting App

A budgeting application designed to support people with ADHD by reducing decision fatigue, creating clear visual cues, and making money management feel approachable instead of overwhelming.

## Overview

This project focuses on building a simple, low-friction budgeting experience for users who may struggle with attention, impulse spending, and long-term planning. The app is centered around immediate clarity, encouraging small wins, and helping users stay consistent without requiring complex financial systems.
