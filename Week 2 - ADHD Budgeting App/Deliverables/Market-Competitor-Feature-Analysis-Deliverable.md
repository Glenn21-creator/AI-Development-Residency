# ADHD Budgeting App: Market, Competitor, and Feature Analysis Deliverable

AI Development Residency, Week 2. This deliverable documents the market research for the ADHD budgeting app in three parts: the market analysis, the competitor analysis (direct and indirect), and the feature comparison against the closest competitors. It builds on the audience segmentation and personas, and everything is written in plain English.

One note up front, so the numbers are read correctly. "ADHD budgeting" is not a market that any research firm tracks on its own, so there is no official market size or growth rate for it. Every figure for the niche is our own estimate, built from the markets that are tracked and scaled by how common ADHD is. Those estimates are labelled as estimates. The figures for the larger parent markets, the app ratings, and the pricing are based on current, cross-checked data.

## 3. Feature Comparison (Most Direct Competitors First)

This section compares our planned app, feature by feature, against the competitors, with the most direct ones first. To keep it readable, the features run down the left and the apps run across the top (the spreadsheet version keeps the template's layout, with apps as rows). The point of the table is to show two things at once: which features are simply expected of any money app (so we have to match them), and which features almost no one offers (so they are where we win).

A few things to keep in mind while reading it. "Our App" is the **planned product**, so its row is a proposal based on this research, not a shipped product. And a rating here only means a feature exists, not that it works well.

**Key**

- **Yes** = clearly offered.
- **Partial** = limited, higher tier only, or a close-but-not-full version.
- **No** = not offered.
- **Unclear** = we could not confirm it.
- **Later** = planned for our product in a later phase, not the first build.

Cake already does auto-allocation and a traffic light, so to beat it we win on the pieces it still lacks, plus a mobile-first build.
